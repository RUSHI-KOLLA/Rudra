## Verbatim Excerpt (Item 5.02)

> On October 12, 2016, John G. Stumpf notified Wells Fargo & Company (the "Company") of his decision to retire as Chairman and Chief Executive Officer and a director of the Company, effective immediately. Also on October 12, 2016, and effective immediately, the Company's Board of Directors (the "Board"): (i) elected President Timothy J. Sloan to the Board and as the Company's Chief Executive Officer; (ii) elected Lead Director Stephen W. Sanger as the Company's non-executive Chairman; and (iii) elected director Elizabeth A. Duke as the Company's non-executive Vice Chair.
>
> Mr. Sloan has served as President and Chief Operating Officer of the Company since November 2015 and as a Senior Executive Vice President of the Company since September 2010, including as Chief Administrative Officer from September 2010 to February 2011, Chief Financial Officer from February 2011 to May 2014, and head of the Wholesale Banking group from May 2014. Mr. Sloan will retain his title as President of the Company. He will not receive any separate compensation for his service as a director of the Company.
>
> The Company's news release announcing these events is attached to this report as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated by reference into this Item 5.02.
>
> Signed: /s/ Anthony R. Augliera, Senior Vice President and Secretary
> Dated: October 12, 2016
