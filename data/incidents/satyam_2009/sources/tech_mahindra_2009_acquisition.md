# Tech Mahindra Acquisition of Satyam — Excerpt

April 13, 2009

Tech Mahindra Ltd. was selected as the successful bidder to acquire a controlling stake in Satyam Computer Services Ltd. through a government-managed bidding process.

The acquisition preserved Satyam as an operating entity and protected approximately 45,000 employees.

Tech Mahindra paid approximately Rs 29 billion for a controlling stake.
