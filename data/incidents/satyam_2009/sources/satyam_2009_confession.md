# Satyam Confession Letter — Excerpt

January 7, 2009

Ramalinga Raju, Chairman of Satyam Computer Services Ltd., wrote to the Board confessing to manipulating the company's accounts.

The letter stated that profits were overstated by approximately Rs 50 billion and that non-existent cash and bank balances of approximately Rs 50 billion were recorded.

The manipulation was described as "riding a tiger, not knowing how to get off without being eaten."
