# BP 2011 Criminal Penalty — Excerpt

March 2, 2011

BP p.l.c. agreed to pay a record $4.5 billion in criminal penalties to resolve charges brought by the U.S. Department of Justice related to the Deepwater Horizon oil spill.

The penalty includes $1.256 billion in criminal fines, the largest ever imposed, and $2.394 billion to the National Fish and Wildlife Foundation and $350 million to the National Academy of Sciences.

BP also agreed to plead guilty to 11 felony counts of misconduct or neglect of ship officers relating to the deaths of 11 workers.

Civil settlements and cleanup costs brought total estimated costs above $65 billion.
