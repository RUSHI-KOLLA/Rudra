# BP 2010 8-K Filing — Excerpt

July 22, 2010

BP p.l.c. announced that Anthony Hayward will step down as Chief Executive Officer, effective October 1, 2010.

Robert Dudley, currently Managing Director of BP's joint venture in Russia (TNK-BP), will succeed Mr. Hayward as Chief Executive Officer.

The Board's decision follows the Deepwater Horizon incident of April 20, 2010 and the ongoing response to the oil spill in the Gulf of Mexico.
