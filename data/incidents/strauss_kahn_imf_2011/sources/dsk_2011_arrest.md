# Strauss-Kahn Arrest Record — Excerpt

May 14, 2011

Dominique Strauss-Kahn, Managing Director of the International Monetary Fund, was arrested at John F. Kennedy International Airport and charged with criminal sexual act, attempted rape, and unlawful imprisonment arising from an incident at a Manhattan hotel.

Mr. Strauss-Kahn was removed from an Air France flight before departure and taken into police custody.

He was arraigned in New York State Supreme Court and remanded without bail.
