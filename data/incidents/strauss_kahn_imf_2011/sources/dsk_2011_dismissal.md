# Strauss-Kahn Charges Dismissed — Excerpt

August 23, 2011

Prosecutors moved to dismiss all criminal charges against Dominique Strauss-Kahn, citing concerns about the credibility of the complainant.

The New York County District Attorney's office stated that "significant problems" with the complainant's credibility made it impossible to prove the charges beyond a reasonable doubt.

Mr. Strauss-Kahn's lawyers had previously challenged the complainant's account, including evidence that she had lied about her immigration history.
