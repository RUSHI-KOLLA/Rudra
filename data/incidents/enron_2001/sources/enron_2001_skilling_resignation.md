# Skilling Resignation — Excerpt

August 15, 2001

Jeffrey Skilling, chief executive of Enron Corp., resigned unexpectedly after just six months in the role. The company said Skilling left for "personal reasons," but the abrupt departure came amid growing concerns about Enron's accounting practices and off-balance-sheet partnerships.

Skilling had joined Enron in 1990 and rose to lead its energy trading and operations divisions before becoming CEO in February 2001. His sudden departure came just months after he replaced Kenneth Lay as CEO.
