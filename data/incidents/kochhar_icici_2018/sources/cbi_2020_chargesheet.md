# CBI Charge Sheet — Excerpt

September 22, 2020

The Central Bureau of Investigation filed a charge sheet against Ms. Chanda Kochhar, her husband Mr. Deepak Kochhar, and others in connection with the sanctioning of credit facilities to the Videocon Group by ICICI Bank.

The charge sheet alleges criminal conspiracy, cheating, and corruption involving approximately Rs 3,250 crore.

The matter is pending before the appropriate court.
