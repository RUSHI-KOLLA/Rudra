# ICICI Bank 2018 Disclosure — Excerpt

October 4, 2018

The Board of Directors of ICICI Bank Limited accepted the resignation of Ms. Chanda Kochhar as Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer.

The Board appointed Mr. Sandeep Bakhshi as Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer.

The resignation follows allegations relating to conflict of interest in the Bank's credit sanctioning process, which are under investigation.
