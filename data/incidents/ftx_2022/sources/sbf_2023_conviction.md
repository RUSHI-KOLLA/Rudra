# SBF Conviction — Excerpt

November 2, 2023

Sam Bankman-Fried, founder of FTX, was convicted on all seven counts of fraud and conspiracy by a federal jury in New York. The jury found that he stole billions of dollars from FTX customers to fund Alameda Research trades, real estate purchases, and political donations. Bankman-Fried faces up to 110 years in prison.
