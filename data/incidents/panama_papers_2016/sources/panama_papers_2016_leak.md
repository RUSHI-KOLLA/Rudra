# Panama Papers Leak — Excerpt

April 3, 2016

The International Consortium of Investigative Journalists published the Panama Papers, a leak of 11.5 million documents from Panamanian law firm Mossack Fonseca. The documents revealed how wealthy individuals and public officials used offshore companies to hide assets and avoid taxes. Among those named was the wife of Icelandic Prime Minister Sigmundur Gunnlaugsson.
