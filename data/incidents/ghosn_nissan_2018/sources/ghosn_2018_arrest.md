# Carlos Ghosn Arrest Record — Excerpt

November 19, 2018

Carlos Ghosn, Chairman and CEO of Nissan Motor Co., Ltd., was arrested by Tokyo prosecutors on suspicion of underreporting his compensation in financial statements filed under Japan's Financial Instruments and Exchange Act.

Prosecutors alleged that Mr. Ghosn underreported his income by approximately 5 billion yen (about $44 million) over eight years.

He was detained in Tokyo while awaiting prosecutorial review.
