# Wirecard Insolvency Filing — Excerpt

June 25, 2020

Wirecard AG filed for insolvency proceedings at the local court in Munich, Germany.

The company disclosed on June 18, 2020 that €1.9 billion in cash purportedly held by a third-party trustee in the Philippines likely did not exist.

Chief Executive Officer Markus Braun was arrested by Munich prosecutors on June 25, 2020.

Former Chief Operating Officer Jan Marsalek disappeared and remains at large.
