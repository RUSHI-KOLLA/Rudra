# BaFin Wirecard Review — Excerpt

July 15, 2020

Germany's financial regulator BaFin conducted a review of its supervision of Wirecard AG following the company's collapse.

The review identified shortcomings in BaFin's oversight and led to the resignation of BaFin President Felix Hufeld.

BaFin announced measures to strengthen its enforcement capabilities, including expanded powers to investigate market manipulation.
