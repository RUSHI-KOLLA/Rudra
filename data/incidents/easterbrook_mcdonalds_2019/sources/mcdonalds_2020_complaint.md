# McDonald's Complaint Against Steve Easterbrook — Excerpt

August 7, 2020

McDonald's Corporation filed a complaint in the Circuit Court of Cook County, Illinois against Stephen Easterbrook, alleging that he materially misrepresented the scope and number of his relationships with company employees during the company's investigation.

The complaint seeks recovery of severance payments and equity awards granted to Mr. Easterbrook following his termination in November 2019.

In 2021, McDonald's reported additional evidence of relationships with at least three more employees not previously disclosed.

The matter was settled in February 2023 with Mr. Easterbrook agreeing to pay McDonald's an undisclosed sum.
