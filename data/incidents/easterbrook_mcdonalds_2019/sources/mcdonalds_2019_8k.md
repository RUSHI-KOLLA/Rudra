# McDonald's 2019 8-K Filing — Excerpt

November 4, 2019

The Board of Directors of McDonald's Corporation terminated the employment of Stephen Easterbrook as President and Chief Executive Officer, effective immediately.

The Board determined, following an investigation conducted with the assistance of outside counsel, that Mr. Easterbrook had violated company policy by engaging in recent consensual relationships with employees.

Mr. Easterbrook's departure was without cause. Chris Kempczinski was named President and Chief Executive Officer.
