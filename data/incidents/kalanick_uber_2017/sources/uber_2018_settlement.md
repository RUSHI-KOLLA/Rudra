# Uber Settlement with Waymo — Excerpt

February 9, 2018

Uber Technologies, Inc. and Waymo LLC announced a settlement of all claims arising from Waymo's lawsuit alleging misappropriation of trade secrets related to self-driving car technology.

Under the terms of the settlement, Uber agreed to pay Waymo approximately $245 million in equity. Uber also agreed to certain restrictions on the use of Waymo's confidential information in its autonomous vehicle development efforts.

The lawsuit had alleged that former Google engineer Anthony Levandowski downloaded thousands of files before leaving to found Otto, which was later acquired by Uber.
