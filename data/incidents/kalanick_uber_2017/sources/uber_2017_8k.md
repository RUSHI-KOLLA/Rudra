# Uber 2017 8-K Filing — Excerpt

June 21, 2017

Travis Kalanick resigned as Chief Executive Officer of Uber Technologies, Inc., effective immediately. Mr. Kalanick will continue to serve as a member of the Board of Directors.

The Board announced that it had undertaken a comprehensive review of the company's culture and leadership practices following multiple allegations of workplace misconduct and the public release of video footage showing Mr. Kalanick in a confrontation with an Uber driver.

Dara Khosrowshahi was subsequently appointed as Chief Executive Officer in August 2017.

In February 2018, Uber Technologies, Inc. entered into a settlement agreement with Waymo LLC resolving all claims relating to alleged misappropriation of trade secrets, with Uber agreeing to pay approximately $245 million in equity.
