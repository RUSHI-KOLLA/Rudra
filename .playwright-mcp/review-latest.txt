## NEW DIRECTION — QUESTION-DRIVEN "MINI CID" INVESTIGATOR (2026-11-20)


1. Why we are superseding the old paste-box + multi-round gap-loop build:
2. Requiring the user to paste an incident summary assumes the user already did the fact-finding. Instead, the system should start from a plain question (for example, why or how something happened) and investigate it like a lean "mini CID" agency.
3. Ten LLM debate personas burn ~16k+ tokens and return ten opinions. We want cheap, mechanical, multi-angle investigation (~1–2k tokens total).
4. Previous commit/evidence drift (overstated diffs, tautological tests, test-stub evidence files) is dropped; every claim going forward must be backed by actual outputs/diffs and strict provenance tags.


5. New Target Architecture (investigate(question)):
6. Entry point: investigate(question) replaces run_match(paste) as the primary flow (keep paste mode only if explicitly decided later).
7. Six investigative perspectives (questions, not personas):
8. Money: Who paid, who benefited, who lost? (filings, contracts, funding, EDGAR)
9. Power: Who appointed, who could remove, who protected? (board records, appointments, regulators)
10. Secrecy: What is reported but unconfirmed? Who won't comment? (press, denials, unnamed sources)
11. Timing: What changed in the weeks before? (dated news, event timelines)
12. Network: Who else is connected to these people or firms? (company registries, sanctions lists)
13. Counter-case: What would have to be true for the official story to be whole? (adversarial queries)
14. Cross-checking: Contradictions across these six lenses (e.g., timing vs. money vs. denials) are the core finding.
15. Cost & Token Discipline: Free search/data sources via sources.py returning {title, url, snippet, date}, mechanical regex/gazetteer extraction for dates/names/amounts, typed structural matching (scoring.py, schema.py v6 with external_pressure added and backfilled), and one bounded LLM synthesis call (~1–2k tokens total).
16. Honesty & Provenance: Every claim keeps its provenance tag (documented only with a real source artifact + verbatim quote; otherwise reported_unconfirmed / inferred / speculative), and paraphrase-backed cards in the 24-card corpus are either cut to the 10 artifact-backed cards or visibly marked as unverified.
